Uploaded receipts and photos
============================

What lands here
  Order confirmations and receipts from Amazon, Best Buy and other sellers for items installed in or left
  in the vehicle, and photographs of those items.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Receipts and photos uploaded on the site" panel on the binder 12
  page are copied into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand.
  Do not rename files written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
  Each item itself is listed in ../../property_loss_items.js; a file in this folder can be referenced from
  an item's "proof" list as "Receipts_And_Photos/Uploaded/<file name>".
