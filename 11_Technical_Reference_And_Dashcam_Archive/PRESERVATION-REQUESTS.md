## 2. Traffic signal timing records
This is the one most likely to actually answer the question of what the left-turn signal was showing.
Modern signal controllers log phase changes and detector actuations.

Send to the **City of Grapevine** (Public Works / Traffic Engineering). Because SH-121 is a state highway,
also send to **TxDOT Dallas District** — the signal may be state-maintained, and you want whichever
agency holds the records. In Texas this is a request under the **Texas Public Information Act**; saying so
starts a formal response clock.

> **Subject:** Texas Public Information Act request — traffic signal records, Bass Pro Dr & SH-121, 2026-08-28
