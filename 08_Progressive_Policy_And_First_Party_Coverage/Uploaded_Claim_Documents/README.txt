Uploaded claim documents
========================

What lands here
  Letters, e-mails and screenshots from either claim file - Progressive claim 26-854858569 (the client's
  own policy) and claim 26-343820011 - uploaded on the site by the client.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Claim documents uploaded on the site" panel on the evidence
  portal are copied into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand.
  Do not rename files written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
