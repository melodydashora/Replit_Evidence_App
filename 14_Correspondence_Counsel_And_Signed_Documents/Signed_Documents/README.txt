Signed documents
================

What lands here
  Engagement letters, authorisations, declarations and other signed papers. This panel takes uploads from
  both the client and counsel.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Signed documents" panel on the binder 14 page are copied into
  this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand. Do not rename files
  written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
