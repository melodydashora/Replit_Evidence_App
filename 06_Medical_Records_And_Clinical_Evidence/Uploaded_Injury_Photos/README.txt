Uploaded injury photographs
===========================

What lands here
  Injury photographs uploaded on the site by the client after the 08/28/2026 collision.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Photographs uploaded on the site" panel on the binder 06 page are
  copied into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand. Do not rename
  files written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
  The captioned gallery on the binder 06 page is built from Pictures_Of_Bruises/ and injury_photos.js;
  this folder holds the copies pulled down from the site's upload store.
