Hertz receipts and screenshots
==============================

What lands here
  Screenshots of the Hertz rental record taken while the rental is running, the final Hertz receipt,
  and any rental agreement, extension or invoice for the rental taken after the 08/28/2026 collision.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Daily screenshots and the final receipt" panel on the binder 13
  page are copied into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand.
  Do not rename files written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
