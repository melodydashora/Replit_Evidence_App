# Claim memo — Uber Optional Injury Protection (Atlantic Specialty Insurance Company)

**Status:** Not yet filed; enrolment unconfirmed · **Contact:** (844) 747-6221, 24 hours
**Notice deadline if enrolled:** 20 days from the injury, i.e. **Thursday, September 17, 2026**

## What this is
A driver injury benefit programme offered through Uber and underwritten by Atlantic Specialty Insurance Company. It is
optional and paid for by the driver, and it pays **only if the driver enrolled before the collision**. The description
on file comes from the evidence and insurance plan in this binder (`UBER-PLAN.md`, 09/01/2026), which records that the
programme covers a driver while online, including while available for requests, en route and on trip, with accident
medical benefits up to $1,000,000 with no deductible, disability earnings replacement up to $500 a week, survivor
benefits up to $150,000, and notice of injury required within 20 days.

**Those terms and that deadline are as stated in that plan. They have not been verified against Atlantic Specialty's
policy wording, and enrolment as of August 28, 2026 has not been confirmed.**

## Assessment
*Analysis prepared for counsel from the documents in this repository. Not legal advice; no attorney has reviewed it.*

This is the one open item on the claims list that can be lost purely by the calendar. Everything else on this case has a
long horizon: the two-year limitations date is August 28, 2028. This one closes on **September 17, 2026** if the
programme applies, and the only way to find out whether it applies is to ask.

The call costs nothing and gives away nothing. Confirming enrolment is a factual question about the client's own
account, not a statement about the collision. If she was enrolled, this is the only source on the whole claims list
that pays her medical costs without a liability fight, without a deductible, and without waiting for the
uninsured-motorist claim to resolve, which matters because personal injury protection is capped at $10,003 and the
hospital balance alone already exceeds it.

If she was not enrolled, the answer takes one phone call and the item closes. Either way, do not let the date pass
unanswered. The related Vehicle Interruption Coverage, if she enrolled in it, requires an in-app crash report within 30
days, i.e. by September 27, 2026, and additionally requires an accepted request at the moment of impact; that report
should not be filed until counsel has approved its wording, because it is a first notice of claim and everything in it
is discoverable.
