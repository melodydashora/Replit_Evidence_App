Binder 13 - Rental Car (Hertz) and Loss of Use
==============================================

What is in this binder
  index.html                      The rental ledger page: the client's ledger of Hertz charges and
                                  payments, and the panel for the daily screenshots and the final receipt.
  Hertz_Receipts_And_Screenshots/ Receipts and screenshots of the rental.

Coverage note
  Progressive's personal policy (claim 26-854858569) carries Rental Reimbursement of $60 per day for up
  to 30 days ($1,800), per the coverage-at-time-of-incident screen in
  08_Progressive_Policy_And_First_Party_Coverage/Claim_26-854858569_And_Policy/.

Uploads
  Files uploaded on the site are copied into Hertz_Receipts_And_Screenshots/ by "npm run pull:uploads".
  Do not rename files that the pull script has written; the name carries the date and the record id.
