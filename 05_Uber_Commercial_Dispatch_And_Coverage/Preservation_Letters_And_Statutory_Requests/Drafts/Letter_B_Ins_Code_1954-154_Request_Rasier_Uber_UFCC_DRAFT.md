Points of wording: (i) the statute has no "on request" language and states no deadline, so the ten-day period is a request, not a statutory time; (ii) the statutory phrase is "insurance claim investigation", used in the letter, rather than "claims coverage investigation"; (iii) the obligors are "a transportation network company and any insurer providing coverage under Subchapter B", and the recipients are "directly interested persons and an insurer of the transportation network company driver"; "directly interested person" is not defined in chapter 1954; (iv) only items (a) and (d) are the two mandated items; items (b), (c), (e) and (f) are framed as additional information; (v) "logged on" is not defined in chapter 1954 (Occ. Code § 2402.004 describes "logged in" for that chapter only); (vi) Occ. Code § 2402.153 uses "passenger", which the letter follows when citing it.
