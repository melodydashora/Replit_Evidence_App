Carrier messages
================

What lands here
  Screenshots of messages, e-mails and letters exchanged with the Progressive claim rep and with
  United Financial Casualty / Progressive Commercial on claim 26-343820011, with the date of each message.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Messages and e-mails with Progressive and the casualty group"
  panel on the binder 14 page are copied into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be
  placed here by hand. Do not rename files written by the pull script; the name carries the document date
  and the record id.
