A. Citizen contact / notice records: all citizen complaints, "Report a (Non-Emergency) Issue" form submissions in the categories "Traffic Signal Issue" and "Street Light Outage (address required)", Traffic Complaint Form submissions, 311/service requests, emails, phone logs, and police-dispatch or CAD entries reporting a signal malfunction, dark/flashing signal, conflicting indication, detection failure, or street-light outage at or near this intersection (Local Schedule GR1000-24 Complaints; PW5275-04 Service and Repair Requests).*
B.
