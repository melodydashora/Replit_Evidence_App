Counsel documents
=================

What lands here
  Documents from counsel. This panel takes uploads from counsel only; the client may download from it.

How files get here
  Files uploaded on the site through the "Documents from counsel" panel on the binder 14 page are copied
  into this folder by "npm run pull:uploads". Files may also be placed here by hand. Do not rename files
  written by the pull script; the name carries the document date and the record id.
