## Assessment
*Analysis prepared for counsel from the documents in this repository. Not legal advice; no attorney has reviewed it.*

**Expect a hospital lien.** The balance is above the $10,003 personal injury protection limit on the client's
Progressive policy, so PIP cannot clear it. In Texas a hospital that treats a patient within 72 hours of an accident
can perfect a lien on the patient's recovery under Property Code chapter 55. Assume that will happen and plan the
settlement distribution around it rather than being surprised by it.

**Get the itemisation before anyone negotiates.** A single portal figure with no service date and no line items cannot
be checked against the records, cannot be reduced, and cannot safely be quoted to an adjuster as the medical special
damages. The itemised statement is also what shows whether the charges are for the emergency visit alone or include
imaging and other services, which matters for the two later emergency visits and any follow-up care.

**Apply for financial assistance now, not after settlement.** Non-profit hospitals must have a financial-assistance
policy, and eligibility is normally assessed on income at the time of application. Applying while the claim is open
costs nothing and can reduce the balance that later has to be paid out of a recovery. Do not pay the account down from
a future settlement without first testing both assistance and lien reduction.

**Do not sign the payment plan reflexively.** $752 a month against an open uninsured-motorist claim converts a
negotiable balance into a fixed obligation and puts a monthly burden on a client whose earnings are interrupted.

## Next steps
1. Request the itemised statement and the service dates in writing.
2. Apply for Baylor Scott & White financial assistance.
3. Ask whether a lien has been filed, and get the lien documents if so.
4. Keep this balance out of any demand figure until the itemisation is in hand.
